# Brand analysis: JIS SHOES NG

Sources reviewed: Telegram channel (157 posts, 637 product photos), Instagram profile @jisstoreng (bio, profile picture, highlight titles). Instagram posts were not reviewed individually.

| Finding | Source | Class | Confidence | Design implication |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Logo: script "jis" over a shoe-sole print, "SHOES.NG" heavy italic caps, "……energized in style" | Instagram profile picture | Observed | High | Redrawn as SVG; used on a dark plate |
| Logo colour gold ≈ #F8B20F | Pixel sample of the logo (JPEG) | Observed (estimate) | Medium | The brand accent |
| No other brand graphics; channel avatar is a photo of a person with shoe boxes | Telegram | Observed | High | Product photos carry the page |
| Catalogue is large and varied: runners, court sneakers, chunky trainers, loafers, derbies, chelsea boots, work boots, slides, clogs | 637 photos | Observed | High | Gallery grouped by style with filters |
| Photos are phone shots, mixed backdrops (grey floors, wood, coloured cushions, boxes), many hand-held | Photos | Observed | High | Neutral white UI so mixed photos don't clash; consistent aspect in grid |
| Many photos show third-party brand boxes and logos | Photos | Observed | High | No brand names in site text (client decision) |
| Voice: warm, celebratory, faith-tinged thank-yous; product posts use a fixed template (price, sizes, fully boxed, how to order) | Posts | Observed | High | Short, direct copy; keep "fully boxed" |
| Ordering habit: customers screenshot a photo and send it on WhatsApp | Captions | Observed | High | Gallery taps send a message with a link to that exact photo |
| Women's styles present (pink sneakers, platform loafers, wedges, pink clogs) | Photos | Observed | High | Gallery mix includes them |
| Target audience | — | Unknown | — | Not stated on the site |

## Preserve, improve, introduce

- **Preserve:** logo and gold; "energized in style"; "fully boxed"; nationwide delivery; WhatsApp ordering.
- **Improve:** the screenshot step (replaced by a pre-filled message with the photo link); finding a style in a long channel (filters by style).
- **Introduce (recommendation):** white catalogue UI with ruled grid; black logo plate; Montserrat italic caps for headings to echo "SHOES.NG".
